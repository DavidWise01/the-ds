NINTENDO DS (DS) — a UD0 EXEREÚNESIS console TEARDOWN + spec edition. The DS bet on two screens — one of them a resistive touchscreen — plus a microphone and Wi-Fi, and quietly became one of the best-selling systems ever. Dual ARM processors ran the show, and the lower touch panel opened games to people controllers had always excluded.
